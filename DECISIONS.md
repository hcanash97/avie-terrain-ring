# Product decisions

Decided by À Vie on 12 November 2026, in response to the reference reconciliation notes.

| Topic | Decision | Status in the build |
|---|---|---|
| Head shape | **Offer both**: an elongated bar (current 12 × 5.5 / 13 × 7.5 / 14 × 10 mm) and a cushion / rounded-square head (about 14–16 mm) | Both built. Bar 12 × 5.5 / 13 × 7.5 / 14 × 10 mm; cushion 10.5 × 9.5 / 12 × 11 / 14 × 13 mm with rounded corners. Customers choose Head shape → Face size. |
| Launch metals | **925 silver, 9ct, 14ct, 18ct and platinum** | All five selectable. Platinum density 20.1 g/cm³ (Pt950) for weight estimates. Supplier pricing and availability not yet confirmed. |
| Shoulder and band choices | **Keep all**: 3 shoulder styles × 3 band profiles | Built. Each combination is covered by the closure tests. |
| Terrain height | **% of maximum relief**, with a "× true scale" readout capped at 30× and a 3 m noise floor | Built and explained on screen under the slider. |

## Still open (need À Vie's own answers, not competitor policy)

- **Authoritative sizing table.** The build uses ISO 8653 arithmetic (UK N = 17.2 mm); a research example used 16.5 mm. Confirm against your caster's mandrels.
- **Lead time, resizing, delivery, certification and hallmarking commitments.** Nothing is promised in the build until these are decided. "Free UK delivery" and "hand finished" were removed for this reason.
- **Pricing model detail.** Currently "priced by written quote". Decide whether live prices appear in the configurator (Phase 14).
- **Proposed brand fonts and palettes in the research.** Not adopted. The build uses the live aviediamonds.com tokens (Trirong, #3F5147, #EFECEC, #252525, #CDA349) plus Inter for compact controls.
