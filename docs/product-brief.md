# Terra product family

À Vie Terra turns a saved geographic terrain design into a jewellery configuration. A terrain version is independent of a product; each physical mount requires its own geometry, constraints, validation and approval.

Current customer product: Terra Ring, an integrated terrain signet. Preserve the established Bar/Cushion and Slim/Standard/Bold presets, metals, finish and sizing. The online preview remains a prototype without live checkout or manufacturing release.

Next: a separate pendant, initially a Cushion with an integrated fixed loop; then a distinct Signet once its differences from the current ring are specified. Catalogue entries exist for cufflinks, charm, tie bar, bracelet, locket and object, but those have no geometry, configuration flow or purchasable variants. Multiple locations, AR, stones, underwater terrain and sculpting remain outside scope.

The Milestone 1 foundation is in `packages/shared-types`, `packages/product-types` and `packages/db`. It defines validated data, not new jewellery meshes or a deployed backend. See [foundation guide](product-family-foundation.md) and [approved staged plan](product-family-plan.md).

A product's declared materials, dimensions and attachments are candidate capabilities, not manufacturing approval. Future products remain unavailable for purchase until geometry, costing, manufacturer acceptance and commerce are implemented.
